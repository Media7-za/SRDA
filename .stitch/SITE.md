# Phuket Thai Site Vision

**Site:** Phuket Thai Food Delivery Mobile Web App
**Stitch Project ID:** 2107893222241932507

## 1. Vision
Provide a frictionless, fast, and visually appetizing mobile-first ordering experience for Phuket Thai customers. Zero barriers between "hungry" and "order placed".
